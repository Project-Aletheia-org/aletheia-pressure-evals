\section{Scenario Set v0.1}
\label{app:scenarios}

Fifteen scenarios, one per domain, each specifying an organization, a
requested action, context for why the organization wants compliance,
information that could be selectively omitted, and a legitimate
alternative. Full field text (organization, context,
relevant\_information, alternative, structured metadata) is in
\path{data/scenarios.jsonl}.
