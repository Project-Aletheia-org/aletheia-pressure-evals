\paragraph{Generation parameters.} Temperature 0.7, top-$p$ 0.9, maximum 3072
new tokens, one sample per cell, random seed 42. The token limit was raised
from 1024 to 3072 during smoke testing, before main generation, because
\texttt{qwen3:4b}'s reasoning exceeded 1024 tokens and produced empty
responses (deviation 1 in \path{reports/protocol_deviations.md}).
